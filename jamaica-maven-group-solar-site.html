Warranty & support

Coverage you can actually read

We tell you plainly what's covered, for how long, and why — panels, batteries, and inverters wear differently, so they're warrantied differently.

25 YR

Solar Panel Performance

Linear performance warranty — panels retain roughly 80–85% of original output through Year 25, degrading at only ~0.5–0.7% a year.

10 YR

Solar Panel Equipment

Covers structural defects — cracked glass, loose junction boxes, frame corrosion — separate from the performance warranty above.

5–10 YR

Batteries & Inverters

The components doing the most electrical work carry a shorter, standard product warranty — we'll walk you through this at point of sale.

What happens if my battery or inverter needs replacing years from now?
If your original model has since been discontinued, JSD Solar supplies a newer, electrically compatible replacement that matches your system's parameters — often an upgrade at no extra cost, not a downgrade.
How do warranty claims work?
Our team submits your component's serial number, photos, and diagnostic data to the manufacturer for verification, then coordinates the replacement — you deal with JMG directly, not an overseas call center.
Is installation included in the system price?
System pricing covers the equipment. Installation and mounting hardware are quoted separately based on your roof and site — we'll confirm this during your free consultation.
Can I size a system for a business, school, or gas station?
Yes — beyond the residential Starter and Full packages, we design custom commercial systems sized to your actual load. Reach out for a site assessment.
